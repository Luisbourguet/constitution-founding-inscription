# Codebook — comparative materials

## traditions_founding_inscription.csv (article § 5.9)
- **Tradition (Glenn):** one of the legal traditions in H. P. Glenn, *Legal Traditions of the World* (5th ed., 2014), plus the supranational case.
- **Point of capture:** where the tradition's founding normative information is first captured on a support (Glenn's "capture").
- **Mode of inscription:** *enacted* (single document issued by a constituent act), *revealed* (text not enacted by any human assembly), *distributed* (spread across statutes, precedents, conventions, memory). Modes overlap and layer within single orders.
- **Technology of hardness:** how the founding inscription is placed beyond ordinary amendment — *textual* (eternity/entrenchment clause), *doctrinal* (judicially declared limit or supremacy), *ritual* (convention, political culture, sacralized practice). Analyse along three dimensions: procedural rigidity, substantive intangibility, effective stability.
- **Exemplary orders:** the constitutional orders used in the article to illustrate the tradition.

## jurisdictions_institutional_comparison.csv (article § 10)
- **Jurisdiction (tradition):** constitutional order and the tradition(s) it draws on.
- **Founding act:** enactment, treaty, revelation or accumulation that founds the order.
- **Intangibility (technology):** the clause, doctrine, procedure or rite that hardens the founding inscription.
- **Recognition of the other:** how the order inscribes recognition of the other (dignity clause, personal laws, land rights, plural constituent subject, etc.), coded on Todorov's axiological, praxeological and epistemic axes.

## ichnological_hierarchy.csv (article § 6)
Levels of inscription following Ferraris's ichnology: trace, record, inscription, founding inscription; each with definition and constitutional correlate.

## quoted_provisions.csv / primary_sources_cited.csv
Verbatim provisions and cited texts, by article and provision; sources as cited in the article. Texts retrieved from Constitute (Comparative Constitutions Project) unless otherwise indicated.
